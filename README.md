# Developer Assessment Platform

**Practice. Test. Track your growth as a developer — all in one place.**

A focused platform for sharpening programming knowledge through multiple-choice
questions, theory questions, code-output questions, and debugging challenges.
Built for candidates who want real practice and admins who want a clean,
reliable way to manage the question bank.

## What This Platform Does

The Developer Assessment Platform gives you a growing bank of programming
questions, organized by subject and topic, and lets you turn that bank into a
quiz in seconds. You answer, get graded instantly, and can review exactly
where you're strong and where you need more reps.

There are two experiences:

- **The Candidate App** — where you practice, take quizzes, and track your progress.
- **The Admin Console** — a separate, protected space where the question bank is curated and kept up to date.

---

## For Candidates

### 🚀 No sign-up, ever
Just open the app and start. There's no account to create, no password to
remember, and nothing tying a quiz you took today to who you are. Your
activity stays private to *your* browser, on *your* device.

### 🧩 Build a quiz your way
Pick a subject, narrow down to specific topics, and choose a difficulty
level. The platform pulls together a fresh set of questions to match — every
time.

### ⚡ Instant, honest grading
The moment you submit, you see your results — no waiting, no server
round-trip. Every question comes with a clear explanation of the correct
answer, so a quiz is also a study session.

### 📊 See your progress
Your dashboard shows your attempt history and highlights **weak areas** —
topics where your accuracy is trending low — so you always know what to
practice next. This is calculated fresh from your own history every time you
look, using thresholds the admin team keeps sensible and up to date.

### 🔖 Bookmark what matters
Save any question you want to revisit later — a tricky debugging problem, a
theory question you want to reread — and find it again in your bookmarks
list any time.

### 🌗 A workspace that stays out of your way
The interface is deliberately black and white: hairline borders, monospace
accents, and line-numbered code blocks that feel like a code editor, not a
quiz app. Dark mode is the default; light mode is one click away.

> **A note on privacy:** because everything — your quizzes, scores, history,
> and bookmarks — lives only in your browser, it won't follow you to another
> device or browser, and clearing your browser data will reset it. There's no
> account to recover it from. This is by design: nothing about your activity
> is ever sent to or stored on a server.

---

## For Admins

**Admin Console** — a protected workspace, separate from the candidate app,
for managing subjects, topics, questions, publishing status, and
platform-wide assessment settings.

It's where the question bank is built and kept healthy — nothing more. There's
no visibility into individual candidates here, because candidate activity is
never sent to a server in the first place.

---

## Design Philosophy

- **Black & white, on purpose.** No color anywhere — just typography, hairline
  borders, and a code-editor-inspired visual language that feels natural to
  developers.
- **Dark mode first.** Light mode is available in one click for anyone who
  prefers it.
- **Privacy by default.** The candidate experience needs no account and
  leaves no server-side trace. What you do on the platform is yours alone.
- **Clarity over cleverness.** Every question includes a real explanation, so
  practicing is also learning.

---

## Privacy & Good to Know

- **Nothing syncs across devices.** Your progress, history, and bookmarks are
  tied to the browser you're using.
- **Clearing your browser data resets everything** — there's no server-side
  backup to restore from, since none of it is stored remotely in the first place.
- **The public question set is fully open.** Explanations and correct answers
  are visible by design — this platform is built for open practice, not
  proctored testing.
